# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the **[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)**
as its code of conduct.

## Our pledge (summary)

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation. We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse,
inclusive and healthy community.

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include empathy and kindness, respect for
differing opinions, giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback, and taking responsibility for
our mistakes. Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or imagery, trolling, insulting or
derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, public or private harassment, and publishing others'
private information without explicit permission.

The full text, including enforcement responsibilities, scope and enforcement guidelines (correction,
warning, temporary ban, permanent ban), is available at the link above and applies to this project in full.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer by opening a
[GitHub issue](https://github.com/vks-eng/multi-cloud-management-platform/issues) marked
**"Code of Conduct"**. If the matter is sensitive, ask in that issue for a private channel and do not post
details publicly. All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with discretion.
